The account-wide community health files for the AllSense repos (GitHub applies them to every public repo of `wen-da-ng` that has none): CONTRIBUTING, CODE_OF_CONDUCT (Contributor Covenant 2.1, unchanged except the contact: allsense@otterbridge.org, a Squarespace forward to the user's inbox), SECURITY (GitHub private vulnerability reporting, switched on per repo by the app repo's `tools/protect_repo.sh`). Public.

## Working here

- **Public:** no serials, IPs, Wi-Fi details, home location, tokens or file paths; never the user's work email.
